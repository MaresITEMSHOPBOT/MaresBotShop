14-day right of withdrawal

You may return any item within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. To do so, email info@mareswear.com with your order number, or use the withdrawal form below.

Conditions

  • Items must be unworn (beyond trying on), unwashed and with all labels.
  • Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item is faulty.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the return (or proof of sending), including the cheapest standard delivery cost you paid.

Faulty items

If your item arrives damaged or develops a defect, contact us — we will repair, replace or refund it, and we cover the return shipping. Claims are resolved within 30 days.

Size exchanges

Need a different size? Email us — we'll arrange an exchange as soon as the original is on its way back.
