## Recommended Print-on-Demand app (connects to Shopify)
For "print only when ordered" (zero waste, no stock), use one of these — both plug
straight into Shopify and sync products, orders and shipping automatically:

- **Printful** ⭐ (best quality, great Shopify integration, EU printing in Latvia/Spain)
- **Printify** (cheaper, more print providers to choose from)

### Setup flow (Printful example)
1. Shopify Admin → **Apps** → search **Printful** → Install.
2. In Printful: **Stores → connect** your Shopify store.
3. **Add product** → pick a heavyweight organic hoodie → upload the front file
   (`01/02/03…png`) to the front and `04_back_planet_eyes.png` to the back.
4. Set the retail price (e.g. €85 / €75 / €89) → Printful shows your profit after print+ship.
5. **Publish to Shopify** → the product appears in your store; when someone buys,
   Printful prints and ships it. You never hold stock. ♻️

## About a "custom app"
You don't need to build a custom app — Printful/Printify *are* the apps that connect
your designs to Shopify and handle printing. If later you want something bespoke
(e.g. a made-to-order configurator), we can look at Shopify's app framework then.
